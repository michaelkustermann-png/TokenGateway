Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026. Applies to the AI Token Payment Gateway service ("the Gateway").

Who operates this service

The Gateway is currently operated by Michael Kustermann as an individual, not yet through a registered company. This policy will be updated to name a formal business entity if and when one is established.

Information we collect

  • Account information -- a label and product identifier you or a client application supply when an account is provisioned, and the account's credit balance.
  • API keys -- we store only a one-way hash of each API key, never the raw key itself, after it's issued to you.
  • Payment information -- purchases are processed entirely by Stripe. We never receive or store your card details. We do receive, from Stripe, confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount, and whatever contact details (e.g. email) you provide during Stripe Checkout.
  • AI request content -- when you make a metered AI request, its content is forwarded to our AI provider (currently OpenAI) to generate a response. We don't otherwise review or retain that content beyond what's needed to process and bill the request.
  • Technical data -- standard server logs (timestamps, error details, IP addresses) generated by operating the service.

How we use this information

  • To provide the service: authenticating requests, metering usage, and billing your account.
  • To maintain an accurate financial ledger of purchases and usage.
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the service.

We do not sell your information to third parties.

Third-party services we rely on

  • Stripe -- payment processing.
  • OpenAI -- fulfilling AI requests. The content of your requests is sent to OpenAI for processing.
  • Supabase -- hosts the database that stores account and transaction records.

Each of these has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.

Data retention

Transaction and ledger records are kept for as long as the associated account exists, as they form the financial record of your balance. You can request deletion of an account and its associated personal information (aside from records we're required to retain for financial record-keeping) by contacting us below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including hashing API keys and using encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed at, and should not be used by, children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service develops. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date above.
